## Research sources behind the rules
- **Session RPE training load** (load = duration in minutes × effort 1–10): Foster C, et al. *A new approach to monitoring exercise training.* J Strength Cond Res. 2001.
- **Subjective check-ins as load indicators**: Saw AE, Main LC, Gastin PB. *Monitoring the athlete training response: subjective self-reported measures trump commonly used objective measures: a systematic review.* Br J Sports Med. 2016.
- **Single-session spike rule** (>10% above the longest session in the last 30 days): Frandsen JSH, et al. *How much running is too much? Identifying high-risk running sessions in a 5200-person cohort study* (Garmin-RUNSAFE). Br J Sports Med. 2025. The study measured running distance. We apply the rule to session duration (see `docs/DECISIONS.md`, D8).

## Data
All data in the app is **synthetic and was generated by us** with `backend/scripts/generate_personas.py` (seeded and deterministic; the story beats come from the team's first dataset in `engine/data/generate.py`). The personas Kasia and Tomek are fictional. No real patient or user data is used.

## Not medical advice
WhyTired does not diagnose and does not recommend medication or lab tests. The doctor summary is a conversation aid for the user's family doctor (POZ).
